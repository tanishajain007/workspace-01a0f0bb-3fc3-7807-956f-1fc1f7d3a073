MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Pick. Place. Play.

A standard game of chess for two people sharing this device. White moves first.

  1. 01

    Find your next move

    Click or tap your piece, then a marked square. You can also drag and drop. Dots mark moves; rings mark captures. A dashed king outline and “!” mean check.

  2. 02

    At your fingertips

    Tab into the board. ↑ ↓ ← → navigate; Enter or Space select. Home/End reach the row edges; add Ctrl for the board edges. Esc cancels.

  3. 03

    The complete game

    Castling, en passant, and all four promotions are included. Illegal moves never change the position. Undo restores everything, including special-move rights and draw counters. Flip only changes your view.

  4. 04

    When the game is drawn

    Stalemate and insufficient material end the game automatically. Threefold repetition and fifty moves by each player without a pawn move or capture allow a draw claim. The claim button also supports a declared next move. Fivefold repetition and seventy-five such moves are automatic draws; checkmate takes precedence.
